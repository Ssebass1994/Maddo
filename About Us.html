OUR VISION

•To empower communities of Masaka Diocese through dairy farming and promote value-added dairy products in East Africa.

MISSION

•To strengthen the dairy supply chain in Masaka Diocese by building a reliable and self-sustaining value-added dairy company.
In so doing, We focus on providing Safe, Nutritious, and Affordable Dairy Products

OUR GOALS

•To offer a reliable market for smallholder dairy farmers in the Greater Masaka Region while improving community nutrition, youth employment, and household incomes.
•To strengthen community and smallholder farmer engagement and promote company visibility. •To maximize profits, widen the financial base, and strengthen financial management.

OUR PROGRAMMES

•We run a Milk Feeding Programme launched in 2024 serving milk to communities & Schools
•We run a Milk collection and processing programme
•We have Dairy Farmer Support & Partnership Projects
•We also have a Cattle Living Loan Programme

SUCCESS STORIES

•To date, 2,443 families have benefited from the program. Increased milk production resulted in the formation of five milk collection centers under respective dairy farmer cooperatives. These centers provide conveniently located collection points for farmers to deliver and sell their raw milk.



FUTURE PROSPECTS

•To boost agricultural extension services and improve collective marketing of quality milk from dairy farmers.
• To increase the processing capacity of MADDO Dairies Ltd.
• To strengthen and enhance sales and marketing strategies.
• To strengthen governance and management capacity of MADDO Dairies Ltd.

HISTORICAL BACKGROUND


In 1993, Caritas MADDO initiated a dairy project by distributing heifers to three farmers through Heifer Project International (HPI), donated by Bothar-lreland.
The primary objective was to improve nutrition, food security, and income among the people of Masaka Diocese. Over time, the number of beneficiaries increased alongside milk production due to intensive training in sustainable agriculture and livestock management.

This progress led to the establishment of a mini dairy in 2003 with support from Heifer Project International and Bothar-lreland. This programme has distributed improved breeds of dairy cows to local families and has boosted household income for years. The recently initiated "Schools Milk Programme" has also so far done a great deal in boosting the health of learners in schools that joined this programme and many other schools have shown interest to join the programme basing on the feedback they get from other schools that are already on the programme.
